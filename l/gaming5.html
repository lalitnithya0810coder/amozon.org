About This Product

The Gaming Mouse Pro is the result of obsessive engineering for competitive players. The PixArt PAW3395 optical sensor tracks at up to 25,600 DPI with zero acceleration, no smoothing, and no prediction — just pure, raw cursor movement that goes exactly where you aim. At just 69 grams with a honeycomb shell, your hand never fatigues during marathon sessions. The 100% PTFE feet glide effortlessly across any mousepad surface, letting you flick and snap with zero resistance. Six programmable buttons can be remapped to any action, keybind, or macro via companion software. Whether you play FPS, MOBA, or RTS, the Gaming Mouse Pro gives you hardware that never gets in the way of your skill.
