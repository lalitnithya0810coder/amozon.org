About This Product

The Cookware Set is built to the standard of professional kitchens and priced for home cooks who refuse to compromise. Riveted stay-cool handles give a secure, confident grip even over high heat, and every piece in the set — including all lids — is fully dishwasher safe. Compatible with every hob type including induction, and oven-safe to 240°C, this is the last cookware set you'll ever need to buy.
