Atomic Habits is the definitive guide to building good habits and breaking bad ones. James Clear draws on neuroscience, psychology and real-world examples to reveal that remarkable results come from tiny, 1% improvements compounded daily. Through his proven Four Laws of Behaviour Change framework, you'll learn to make good habits obvious, attractive, easy, and satisfying — while making bad habits invisible, unattractive, difficult, and unsatisfying.

What You'll Learn

01

The 1% Rule

Discover why getting 1% better every day leads to being 37 times better over a year — and how marginal gains transform every area of your life.

02

Four Laws of Behaviour Change

A simple, elegant framework: Make it Obvious, Make it Attractive, Make it Easy, Make it Satisfying. Four laws to build any habit you desire.

03

Identity-Based Habits

Real change comes from changing your identity first. Learn to stop focusing on outcomes and start focusing on who you wish to become.
