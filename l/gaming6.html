About This Product

The Gaming Headset 7.1 immerses you completely in the world of your game. Virtual 7.1 surround sound, powered by the companion audio software, pinpoints the direction of every footstep, gunshot, and explosion with millimetre accuracy — giving you critical information before your enemies even know you're there. The 50mm neodymium drivers produce deep, rumbling bass and crystal-clear highs across a full 20Hz–20KHz range. Memory foam leatherette ear cushions seal out the room and keep your ears comfortable through hours of play. The retractable noise-cancelling microphone picks up your voice cleanly and eliminates background keyboard clicks and room noise. Plug in via USB for surround sound or 3.5mm for console and mobile — one headset for every platform you play on.
