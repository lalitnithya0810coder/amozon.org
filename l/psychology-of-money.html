Doing well with money isn't necessarily about what you know — it's about how you behave. Morgan Housel shares 19 short stories exploring the strange ways people think about money and teaches you how to make better sense of one of life's most important topics. From understanding the role of luck and risk to the power of compounding and the value of saving without a specific goal — this book reveals the timeless lessons about wealth, greed, and happiness that no spreadsheet can teach.

No One's Crazy

People's money decisions are shaped by their unique experiences. What seems irrational to you makes perfect sense to someone who lived a different financial life.

02

Wealth Is What You Don't See

Wealth is the expensive cars not purchased, the watches not worn, the clothes not bought. True wealth is hidden — it's financial options and freedom, not visible spending.

03

The Power of Compounding

Warren Buffett's skill is investing — but his secret is time. Good investing isn't about earning the highest returns; it's about earning very good returns for the longest period.

Financial success is not a hard science. It's a soft skill, where how you behave is more important than what you know.
