A timeless fable about following your dreams. Santiago, a young Andalusian shepherd, sets out on a journey from Spain to the Egyptian desert in search of a treasure buried near the Pyramids. Along the way he meets a Gypsy woman, a mysterious king, and an Alchemist — all of whom point him toward his "Personal Legend." Coelho's enchanting tale has sold over 150 million copies, been translated into 80 languages, and holds the Guinness World Record as the most translated book by a living author. A profoundly simple yet deeply moving story about listening to your heart and trusting the universe.

Themes That Resonate

✦

The Personal Legend

Coelho introduces the idea that every person has a "Personal Legend" — a unique purpose they are destined to fulfil. The universe conspires to help those who pursue it wholeheartedly.

✦

The Language of the World

Beyond words and borders lies a universal language understood by all — the language of the heart. Santiago learns to read omens and listen to the Soul of the World.

✦

Treasure Is Within

The greatest journey is inward. What Santiago discovers at the end of his quest is not what he expected — but everything he needed. A truth as ancient as alchemy itself.

When you want something, all the universe conspires in helping you to achieve it.
